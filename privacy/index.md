# Snore Snagger – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Snore Snagger is an iPhone app that records your night with the microphone, finds snoring in the recording and shows a Snore score, a graph of the night and short snoring clips. It is made by Andreas Schweikert, an individual developer in Zurich, Switzerland ("I" below).

## In short

Recording and analysis happen on your iPhone. Snore Snagger has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Snore Snagger does not automatically upload recordings or clips. Sharing a report sends its included audio to the recipients you choose.

## What the app records and keeps

With your permission, the app uses the microphone only while you record a night (also while the screen is locked). The recording can contain snoring, breathing, speech and other sounds in the room, including other people.

The app analyses the recording on your iPhone and keeps the night's result there: the date and times, the Snore score, the graph and short clips of about 25 seconds.

By default, the full recording is deleted once the result and clips are saved and checked. In Settings you can choose to keep full recordings; they then stay on your iPhone. If an analysis fails or is cancelled, the recording stays until you try again or delete the night.

You can delete a night at any time; this removes its recording, result and clips. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. The app's recordings and nights are not included in iCloud or computer backups.

## Sharing a report

You can share a report of a night: one file with its date and times, Snore score, graph and audio clips. It is sent only when you choose, through the iPhone's share sheet, to the people or apps you pick (for example Mail, Messages or Files). Anyone who receives it can listen to the clips. The app you share with handles the file under its own terms.

## When you send me a report (beta testing)

During the beta I may ask you to send me a report as feedback, for example by e-mail. This is voluntary. If you do, I receive the report with its audio clips and your e-mail address or phone number from the message. I use it only to improve how the app detects snoring and works, I do not pass it on, and I delete it when it is no longer needed for that, at the latest 12 months after the beta ends. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

## TestFlight

If you test the app with Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects data and shares some of it with me: your name and e-mail address, your device model and iOS version, crash reports, usage information (such as installs and sessions) and any feedback and screenshots you send through TestFlight. See Apple's [TestFlight & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/). I use it only to run the beta and fix problems.

## Not a medical device

The Snore score describes the snoring sounds the app detected. Snore Snagger does not diagnose sleep apnoea or any other condition.

## Children

Snore Snagger is not meant for children under 13.

## Your rights and contact

For questions, or to see or delete anything you sent me, write to oblomow@hotmail.com. Depending on where you live, data protection law (for example the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection or the EU General Data Protection Regulation) gives you rights to access, correct and delete your data and to complain to a data protection authority (in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, FDPIC).
